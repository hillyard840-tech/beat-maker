# Review Response Writer

Only for the owner or staff. Never for customers, and never post replies yourself; the owner copies and posts them.

## Inputs
The review text, star rating, platform, reviewer's first name if shown, and anything the owner knows about what happened.

## Procedure
1. Classify: positive (4–5★), mixed (3★), negative (1–2★), or suspicious (no record of the customer, competitor, spam).
2. Draft the reply using the pattern below, in the voice from `business/business-facts.md`.
3. Give one draft, plus a shorter alternative for negative reviews.
4. Flag anything the owner should check before posting (facts you assumed, an offer you included).

## Patterns
- **Positive**: thank them by name, mention one specific detail from their review, a short warm line about the team or product, an invitation back. 2–3 sentences. Vary wording; don't reuse the same reply.
- **Mixed**: thank them, acknowledge the issue specifically, say what's changing (only if the owner confirmed), invite them back.
- **Negative**: apologise for their experience without arguing, acknowledge the specific issue, don't share private details or order numbers, move it offline: "Please contact us at <email/phone> so we can put this right." 3–4 sentences. Calm and professional; future customers are the real audience.
- **Suspicious**: polite and factual: "We can't find a record of your visit, but we'd like to understand what happened. Please contact us at…". Suggest the owner reports it through the platform if it breaks the platform's rules.

## Never
- Argue, blame the customer, or reveal private information (health, order contents, history).
- Offer compensation publicly unless the owner says so.
- Stuff keywords or add details that did not happen. Don't ask for reviews to be changed in exchange for anything (most platforms ban it).
