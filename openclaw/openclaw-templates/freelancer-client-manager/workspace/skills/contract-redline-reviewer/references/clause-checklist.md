# Clause checklist by contract type

Use the "All contracts" list for every review, then add the list for the specific type. A clause being absent is a finding only when its absence hurts the party the user represents.

## All contracts
- Parties correctly named, with legal entity names (not trading names only)
- Effective date and term
- Renewal: automatic or manual, notice period to stop renewal
- Termination for convenience (either party, notice period)
- Termination for cause, with a cure period
- What happens on termination: payment for work done, return or deletion of data, surviving clauses
- Payment amount, currency, invoicing, due date, late-payment interest or right to suspend
- Limitation of liability: cap amount, mutual or one-sided, carve-outs
- Exclusion of indirect and consequential damages
- Indemnities: who, for what, capped or uncapped, procedure (notice, control of defence)
- Confidentiality: definition, exclusions, duration, permitted disclosures
- Governing law and dispute forum (courts or arbitration, location)
- Assignment and change of control
- Force majeure
- Notices: how and where formal notices are sent
- Entire agreement and order of precedence between documents
- Amendments must be in writing
- Severability

## Mutual or one-way NDA
- Mutual or one-way, and does that match reality
- Definition of Confidential Information: marked only, or everything
- Standard exclusions: already known, public, independently developed, received from a third party
- Purpose limitation
- Term of the NDA and how long the duty survives (2 to 5 years typical; trade secrets can be longer)
- Return or destruction on request, with a carve-out for routine backups
- Compelled disclosure (subpoena) procedure
- No licence granted, no obligation to do a deal
- Non-solicitation or non-compete hidden inside an NDA (flag it; it rarely belongs there)
- Residuals clause (lets the other side use what they remember; often High for the discloser)

## Master services agreement / consulting / freelance
- Scope defined by SOW, and a change-request process
- Acceptance criteria and deemed acceptance after X days
- IP ownership: who owns deliverables, when ownership transfers (on payment is better for the provider)
- Pre-existing IP and tools retained by the provider, with a licence to the client
- Right to show work in a portfolio (for freelancers)
- Payment milestones, deposit, kill fee
- Right to suspend work for non-payment
- Expenses: approval and reimbursement
- Independent contractor status (no employment relationship)
- Non-solicitation of staff: duration, mutual
- Non-compete (often High for an individual freelancer)
- Warranty period and remedy (re-perform vs. refund)
- Insurance requirements proportionate to the contract size

## SaaS / software subscription (user is usually the customer)
- Service level: uptime commitment, measurement, service credits, sole-remedy language
- Data ownership: customer owns customer data
- Data export on termination, in a usable format, with a time window
- Data protection terms or DPA, sub-processors list, breach notification timing
- Security commitments
- Price increases on renewal: capped or not
- Suspension rights: when the vendor can cut off service
- Vendor's right to change features or terms unilaterally
- Use of customer data for training AI models or analytics
- IP indemnity from the vendor (third-party infringement claims)
- Auto-renewal notice window and how to cancel

## Commercial or residential lease
- Rent, review or escalation mechanism, deposit amount and return conditions
- Repair obligations: who fixes what
- Service charges and how they are calculated or capped
- Permitted use
- Break clause and its conditions
- Subletting and assignment
- Reinstatement / dilapidations at end of term
- Landlord's access rights and notice
- Insurance responsibilities

## Employment offer or contract (user is usually the employee)
- Title, duties, location, hours
- Salary, bonus basis, equity terms (vesting, cliff, acceleration)
- Probation and notice periods
- Restrictive covenants: non-compete, non-solicit, duration and geography
- IP assignment scope: only work-related, or everything including personal projects
- Garden leave, payment in lieu of notice
- Clawback provisions
